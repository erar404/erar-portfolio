# PRODUCT.md

register: brand

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio for Erwin Roy Arellano, a full-stack software engineer in Quezon City, Philippines, with 6+ years of experience. The site exists to get him hired or contracted: it shows what he has built (enterprise systems for RGMC Group, HR platform work at Titanium Systems / MyHR, personal products for music venues and bands) and lets a visitor start a conversation via a contact form.

## Users
Hiring managers, tech leads, recruiters, and potential clients reviewing a candidate on a laptop, usually during working hours, skimming quickly. Secondary: peers and collaborators checking his GitHub work.

## Brand voice
Three physical-object words: **machined, warm, dependable.** Like a well-used brass tool on a workbench: precise, a little industrial, but human. Not startup-slick, not agency-artsy.

## Tone
Direct, first-person, confident without bragging. Short sentences. Names the concrete thing built rather than adjectives about it.

## Anti-references
- Generic "developer portfolio" template: centered hero, three icon cards, skill bars with percentages.
- Neon-on-black "hacker" aesthetic; matrix rain; terminal cosplay.
- Purple-gradient SaaS look; glassmorphism cards; gradient text.
- Editorial magazine look with italic serif headlines and rule-separated columns.

## Strategic principles
1. Evidence over adjectives: every skill claim is backed by a named system with a real screenshot or repo link.
2. Enterprise work first: the RGMC Group systems carry the most weight, then Titanium/MyHR, then personal products.
3. One long, well-paced scroll; every section is reachable from a persistent nav.
4. Motion is choreography, not decoration: staggered reveals on entry, scroll-linked progress, restrained hover.
5. Works on a phone, but designed for a 13-inch laptop screen first.
